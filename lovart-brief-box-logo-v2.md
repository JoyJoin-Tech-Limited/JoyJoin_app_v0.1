# Lovart Brief — JoyJoin Box Logo Iteration (v2)

## Goal
Refresh the JoyJoin box logo to align with the current canonical 2D low-poly illustration style (插画风), replacing the current 3D-render appearance with a softer, painterly 2D aesthetic. Produce both a **primary logo** and a **simplified icon variant** for small-size use.

## Concept (preserve)
- A purple gift box as the central metaphor — surprise, warmth, gathering
- Three canonical archetype animals peeking out from the box:
  - **气氛组柯基 (Corgi)** — warm orange/white, playful, front-left
  - **情绪树洞考拉 (Koala)** — soft gray, gentle expression, back-right
  - **慢半拍龟 (Turtle)** — fresh green, calm, front-right
- Chinese characters **"悦聚"** on the box faces (front and right side)

## Style (插画风统一 — MANDATORY)

**Construction:** 2D digital illustration with low-poly / geometric faceted aesthetic — NOT a 3D render. The box and animals should feel like flat illustrations that use polygonal facets to suggest dimension, not actual dimensional models.

**Textures:** Painterly, soft brushed feel within each polygonal facet. Avoid glossy 3D shading. Use matte, textured surfaces.

**Outlines:** Minimal or none — let facet edges define form. No thick black outlines.

**Gradients:** Soft color variation within individual facets, not global smooth gradients.

**Background:** Transparent (PNG with alpha) for the primary logo; optional warm beige #F5F1E8 wash for marketing variants.

**Characters:**
- Geometric polygonal bodies, large expressive glossy eyes, simplified features
- Warm, friendly expressions — approachable, not hyper-cute
- Each animal's color palette should feel natural and warm:
  - Corgi: warm orange #FF9B85 tones, cream white
  - Koala: soft gray-lavender #ADABBC tones
  - Turtle: fresh green #9ACD32 tones with shell pattern detail

**Box:**
- Vibrant Purple #8B5CF6 as the primary box color
- Slight facet variation within the purple (darker/lighter polygonal planes)
- "悦聚" characters in Soft White #FFFFFF or Warm Beige #F5F1E8
- Characters should feel hand-drawn, rounded, friendly — matching the AlimamaFangYuanTiVF rounded geometric feel

**Color lock:**
- Primary: Vibrant Purple #8B5CF6 (box)
- Accents: Warm Coral #FF9B85 (corgi), Fresh Green #9ACD32 (turtle), Sky Blue #A8C5DD or gray-lavender #ADABBC (koala)
- Text: Soft White #FFFFFF / Warm Beige #F5F1E8
- Never use neon, pure black, or harsh contrast

## Mood
Warm, playful, surprising, premium-but-approachable. Like opening a gift with friends.

## Composition
- Centered gift box, slightly isometric angle (not full 3D perspective)
- Animals arranged in a layered group: corgi forward-left, koala slightly behind, turtle forward-right
- Generous negative space around the group
- Circular vignette feel — the animal group forms a natural rounded cluster

## Anti-generic test
Could this exact illustration appear in a generic social app? No — the low-poly painterly construction, the specific animal trio, the purple box with "悦聚" characters, and the facet-edge-defined forms should make it unmistakably JoyJoin.

## Variations requested

### A. Primary logo
- Full detail with all three animals and "悦聚" characters
- Transparent background PNG
- Dimensions: 1600×1600px minimum

### B. Simplified icon variant
- Same animals and box, but reduced detail for small sizes (app icon, favicon, mini-program icon)
- "悦聚" characters may be simplified to a single "悦" or removed if illegible below 120×120px
- Animals should remain recognizable at 64×64px
- Transparent background PNG
- Dimensions: 512×512px, 1024×1024px

### C. Color-background adaptability check
- One version on Warm Beige #F5F1E8 background
- One version on white #FFFFFF background
- One version on Vibrant Purple #8B5CF6 background (inverted/lightened elements)

## Export
- PNG with transparency for all variants
- File naming: `lovart-box-logo-v2-{variant}-{date}-v1.png`
- Provide 1x and 2x resolutions

## Reference
Match the painterly softness and facet treatment of the existing Lovart corgi welcome asset (`lovart-mascot-corgi-welcome-20260422-v1`), but applied to a multi-character group composition.
