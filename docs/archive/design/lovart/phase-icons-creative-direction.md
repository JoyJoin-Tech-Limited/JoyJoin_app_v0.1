# Phase Icons — Creative Direction (High-Level)

> One sentence per phase. One dominant subject. Two archetype framers. One color family.

---

## The Unified Formula

```
[DOMINANT SUBJECT] + [2 ARCHETYPES framing] + [COLOR FAMILY]
    ↓
Must read as the phase name when squinted to 80px.
```

**Composition rule:** Subject in center (~60%). Archetypes peek from edges (~30%). Sparkles/details fill gaps (~10%).

---

## The 10 Phases

### 1. warmup (热身)
**One-liner:** A sunrise that feels like a friendly invitation.
- **Subject:** Sun — circular, warm, radiating gentle rays
- **Framers:** Corgi (bottom left, welcoming) + Rooster (top right, crowing at dawn)
- **Palette:** Amber, gold, soft coral
- **Emotion:** "The night is starting, come on in."

---

### 2. micro_challenge (挑战)
**One-liner:** A puzzle that wants to be solved.
- **Subject:** Puzzle piece or trophy — geometric, satisfying
- **Framers:** Fox (left, paw on piece, clever grin) + Spider (top right, web threads connecting)
- **Palette:** Cyan, blue, cool indigo
- **Emotion:** "Aha! That clicked."

---

### 3. lie_detective (谎言侦探)
**One-liner:** A mask hiding a lie behind a smile.
- **Subject:** Theatrical mask with one half slightly shadowed or cracked, revealing subtle mismatch
- **Framers:** Owl (right, peering wisely) + Fox (left, clever grin, deceptive)
- **Palette:** Purple, violet, soft lavender wash
- **Emotion:** "Something here doesn't match..."

---

### 4. personality_dice (人格骰子)
**One-liner:** A die that knows something about you.
- **Subject:** Multi-faceted die or crystal ball showing trait colors
- **Framers:** Octopus (bottom, tentacles holding dice) + Hamster (top, cheering)
- **Palette:** Pink, fuchsia, playful purple
- **Emotion:** "What will fate reveal?"

---

### 5. auction (拍卖)
**One-liner:** A gavel about to drop on something ridiculous.
- **Subject:** Gavel mid-strike or spotlighted podium with coins
- **Framers:** Rooster (right, wing raised like auctioneer) + Elephant (left, steady, counting)
- **Palette:** Gold, amber, bronze
- **Emotion:** "Going once... going twice..."

---

### 6. quip_battle (机智对决)
**One-liner:** A lightbulb that just thought of something stupid-funny.
- **Subject:** Lightbulb with playful spark lines, or speech bubble with zigzag energy lines
- **Framers:** Fox (left, witty smirk) + Corgi (bottom right, laughing)
- **Palette:** Yellow, orange, warm amber
- **Emotion:** "Wait, hear me out..."

---

### 7. undercover_word (谁是卧底)
**One-liner:** Two cards that look the same but aren't.
- **Subject:** Magnifying glass over TWO nearly identical cards with a subtle visual difference (one slightly askew, different corner fold, or mismatched shadow)
- **Framers:** Owl (top right, observant) + Spider (bottom left, weaving a web of deception)
- **Palette:** Red, rose, soft burgundy
- **Emotion:** "Who's the odd one out?"

---

### 8. group_mirror (群像镜像)
**One-liner:** A mirror that shows who you really are to your friends.
- **Subject:** Round mirror or prism reflecting soft abstract silhouettes and colorful shape echoes
- **Framers:** Dolphin (left, empathetic, reads the room) + Koala (bottom right, warm hug)
- **Palette:** Teal, cyan, sky blue
- **Emotion:** "This is how we see you."

---

### 9. mini_script (迷你剧本杀)
**One-liner:** A script scroll that pulls you into a story.
- **Subject:** Unfurling blank parchment scroll with wax seal and ribbon, or stage spotlight beam cutting through a velvet curtain
- **Framers:** Cat (right, dramatic pose, paw on script) + Owl (left, narrating)
- **Palette:** Indigo, slate, deep purple
- **Emotion:** "You are the character now."

---

### 10. recap (回顾)
**One-liner:** A gentle trophy made of memories.
- **Subject:** Floating memory bubbles or soft trophy cup with hearts
- **Framers:** Calm Dolphin (top left, peaceful) + Turtle (bottom right, content)
- **Palette:** Violet, soft purple, warm gold highlights
- **Emotion:** "We did this together."

## The 80px Test

For each icon, ask: **If I saw this at the size of a fingernail, would I know which phase it is?**

If the answer is no, the dominant subject is too small or too complex. Simplify the subject. Push the characters further to the edges.
