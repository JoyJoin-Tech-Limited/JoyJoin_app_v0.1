# Lovart Design Brief: Social Icebreaker Phase Icons

> **Goal:** Produce 7 proprietary phase header icons + 5 rating face icons to replace emoji placeholders in the mini-program Social Icebreaker experience.
> **Tool:** Lovart AI Design Agent (ChatCanvas)
> **Style:** 2D low-poly geometric illustration (插画风)
> **Delivery:** SVG source + PNG at 1×/2×/3×

---

## Brand Parameters (Inject into Every Prompt)

- **Primary:** Vibrant Purple `#8B5CF6`
- **Warm highlight:** Warm Coral `#FF9B85`
- **Cool accent:** Sky Blue `#A8C5DD`
- **Positive signal:** Fresh Green `#9ACD32`
- **Background/base:** Warm Beige `#F5F1E8`
- **Typography feel:** Rounded Chinese display (AlimamaFangYuanTiVF), soft geometric English (Quicksand)
- **Visual tone:** Warm, cute but tasteful, rounded and soft, lively and breathable, minimal yet refined

**Style lock (插画风统一 — MANDATORY):**
- 2D digital illustration with low-poly / geometric faceted aesthetic
- Built from polygonal facets and triangular planes
- Painterly, soft brushed feel within each facet — NOT flat vector
- Minimal or no outlines — facet edges define form
- Soft color variation within individual facets
- Natural warm palette with controlled purple accent
- Transparent background
- No whole-icon gradients
