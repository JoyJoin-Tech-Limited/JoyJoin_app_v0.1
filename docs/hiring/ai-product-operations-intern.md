# AI Product Operations Intern

**Location:** Remote / On-site
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks (with extension option)
**Team:** Product & Engineering

---

## About JoyJoin

JoyJoin is an AI-powered social discovery platform. We are pre-launch and building toward our beta release. Our product spans WeChat Mini Program, admin portal, and AI backend systems — personality matching, icebreaker generation, venue logistics, and payment.

## Role Summary

You will be the connective tissue between testing, engineering, and product decisions. You own the beta launch readiness tracker, prioritize the fix backlog, run the weekly bug council, and ensure that every bug found by the Evaluation team gets triaged, assigned, and either fixed or consciously deferred. You translate raw bug reports into actionable product decisions.

## What You'll Do

- Build and maintain the **beta launch readiness tracker** — a single source of truth for what must work before launch
- Run the **weekly bug council**: gather bug reports from the Evaluation team, facilitate severity/sizing discussion, produce a prioritized fix backlog
- Triage incoming bugs: classify by area (onboarding / matching / icebreaker / payment / admin), severity (blocker / critical / major / minor), and reproducibility
- Track fix velocity — are we fixing faster than they're finding? Escalate when the curve goes the wrong way
- Produce a **weekly product signal report** for the founding team: top 5 bugs, top 5 user friction points, trend vs last week
- Make go/no-go recommendations per release candidate based on regression results
- Own the iteration loop: bug found → triaged → fixed → verified → closed

## What We're Looking For

- Currently pursuing a degree in Product Management, CS, or equivalent practical experience
- Strong organizational skills — you can maintain a tracker that people trust
- Ability to synthesize many inputs into a clear recommendation
- Technical enough to understand bug reports, read API responses, and ask engineers the right questions
- Clear written communication in English and Chinese
- Bonus: experience with GitHub Issues / Projects, Notion, Linear, or any bug tracking tool; prior PM internship; familiarity with AI products

## What You'll Gain

- Real experience shipping an AI product from pre-launch to beta
- Product sense — learn what "good enough to ship" really means
- Your name in beta release credits
- Direct mentorship from founding team on product strategy and prioritization
- Strong signal for AI Product Operations / PM roles at AI-native companies
