# AI Evaluation Engineer Intern

**Location:** Remote / On-site
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks (with extension option)
**Team:** Product & Engineering

---

## About JoyJoin

JoyJoin is an AI-powered social discovery platform connecting young people through shared interests, personality matching, and curated in-person events. Our core AI systems power personality assessment, matching algorithms, game design generation, and icebreaker experiences. We are pre-launch and building the foundation for our beta release.

## Role Summary

You will be the primary quality signal for JoyJoin's user-facing AI products. You will design and execute structured test campaigns across the WeChat Mini Program, covering the full user journey from registration through matching to icebreaker sessions and post-event feedback. You will work directly with AI-powered features — personality assessment, lie detection games, AI-generated challenges, and matching explanations — and produce high-signal bug reports with repro steps, severity, and edge cases.

## What You'll Do

- Own end-to-end test coverage for a vertical slice of the mini-program (onboarding → matching → discover, or icebreaker sessions → payment → post-event)
- Design structured test campaigns around AI feature boundaries (personality quiz accuracy, matching result quality, icebreaker phase transitions, LLM-generated content consistency)
- File structured bug reports: repro steps, expected vs actual behavior, severity, environment, screen recordings
- Test across device profiles (iPhone notch, Android punch-hole, low-end devices, varying network conditions)
- Identify edge cases in AI-generated outputs — hallucinated content, inconsistent tone, broken state transitions
- Collaborate with the AI Ops and Product Ops interns on regression tracking and fix verification
- Work with engineering to reproduce and isolate bugs

## What We're Looking For

- Currently pursuing a degree in CS, EE, HCI, or equivalent practical experience
- Systematic thinking — you don't just "click around", you design coverage
- Familiarity with mobile app testing (WeChat Mini Program experience is a strong plus)
- Attention to detail — you notice when an animation skips, a state doesn't transition, or a prompt returns Chinese when it should return English
- Comfort filing structured bug reports (you understand what makes a repro actionably vs "it doesn't work")
- Bonus: experience with LLM product testing, prompt evaluation frameworks, or automated testing (Playwright, Appium)

## What You'll Gain

- Real experience testing production AI systems pre-launch
- Your name in beta release credits
- Deep understanding of AI product lifecycle from development to launch
- Direct mentorship from founding engineers
- Strong signal for AI Evaluation / QA Engineer roles at Scale AI, ByteDance, OpenAI, Anthropic
