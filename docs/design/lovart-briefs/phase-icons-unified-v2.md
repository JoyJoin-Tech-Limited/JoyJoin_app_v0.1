## Strategic Context

We are **redrawing all 10 phase icons** from scratch in a unified visual language. The previous 7 icons were designed as small 80×80px navigation markers. The new approach treats each phase icon as a **miniature hero illustration** — readable at 80px, impressive at 240px.

**Why redraw all 10?** Brand coherence. Old and new icons side by side in a grid must feel like siblings, not distant cousins.

---

## The Adaptive Composition Rule (MANDATORY)

Every phase icon must obey this design rule:

```
DOMINANT SUBJECT (center, 60% of visual weight)
    ↓
Must be instantly recognizable at 80×80px
Examples: magnifying glass, lightbulb, sun, die, gavel

ARCHETYPE CHARACTERS (frame, 30% of visual weight)
    ↓
2–3 JoyJoin archetype animals peeking from edges
At 80px: read as warm texture / framing
At 240px: recognizable faces with expressions

ATMOSPHERIC DETAILS (fill, 10% of visual weight)
    ↓
Sparkles, hearts, stars, light particles
At 80px: add texture without clutter
At 240px: tell the phase's micro-story
```

**Test:** Squint at the image until it's 80px in your mind. Can you still tell what phase it is? If no → dominant subject needs to be bigger or simpler.

## Style Lock (MANDATORY — paste into every Lovart prompt)

```
Style: 2D digital illustration with low-poly / geometric faceted aesthetic.
Textures: Painterly, soft brushed feel within each polygonal facet — NOT flat vector or 3D render.
Outlines: Minimal or none — let facet edges define form.
Gradients: Soft color variation within individual facets, not global gradients.
Background: Transparent PNG (no background fill). Subject centered with soft circular vignette edge.
Characters: Geometric polygonal bodies, large expressive glossy eyes, simplified features, warm expressions.
Composition: Adaptive — one dominant subject (60% weight), 2-3 archetype characters framing (30%), atmospheric details (10%).
Color treatment: Natural warm palette; brand purple #8B5CF6 for key elements only.
Anti-generic test: Could this exact illustration appear in a generic dating app? If yes → iterate.
Readability test: Must be recognizable as its phase when imagined at 80×80px.
```
